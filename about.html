Guidance and Instructions for Team Members

1. Communication

Always communicate clearly and professionally with your teammates and clients. Respond to messages within 24 hours.

2. Work Hours

Official working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Notify your team leader if you will be unavailable.

3. Task Management

All tasks must be logged and updated regularly. Mark tasks as complete once they are done and inform your supervisor.

4. Code of Conduct

Treat every team member with respect. Discrimination, harassment, or unprofessional behavior will not be tolerated.

5. Deadlines

Always meet your deadlines. If you are unable to complete a task on time, inform your team leader as early as possible.

6. Confidentiality

Do not share any company or client information with unauthorized persons. Protect all sensitive data at all times.
